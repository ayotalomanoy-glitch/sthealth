Why "Sthealth" Stretch?

Traditional break reminders hijack your screen right in the middle of a thought or coding sprint, forcing you to rage-quit the app. We engineered the exact opposite.

Traditional Break Timers

  • Suddenly blocks your entire screen while you are typing
  • Steals keyboard focus and breaks your train of thought
  • Forces long breaks when you just want to finish one line
  • Ends up getting disabled or uninstalled within 3 days

Sthealth Stretch (Stealth + Health)

  • OS-Level Idle Detection: Waits until your keyboard & mouse completely pause
  • Zero Focus Stealing: Pops up quietly in the corner without interrupting typing
  • Elapsed Overdue Counter: Gently counts up to nudge you when you're ready
  • 1 Stretch at a Time: Takes just 20 seconds right at your desk

Clear visual guides with recommended durations (seconds) or repetitions. Do just one stretch per reminder to feel a dramatic difference by evening.
