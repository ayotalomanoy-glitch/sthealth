Why "Sthealth" Stretch?

Traditional break reminders hijack your entire screen while you are deep in thought, forcing you to disable the app. We engineered the gentle opposite.

Traditional Break Reminders

  • Suddenly blocks your screen right in the middle of typing
  • Steals active window focus and breaks your concentration
  • Forces long interruptions when you just want to finish a sentence
  • Ends up getting muted or uninstalled after 3 days

Sthealth Stretch (Stealth + Health)

  • OS-Level Idle Detection: Waits until your hands pause naturally
  • Zero Focus Stealing: Pops up gently without interrupting your typing
  • Elapsed Overdue Counter: Softly counts up so you stretch when ready
  • Quick 20-Second Resets: Just one simple desk stretch per reminder
